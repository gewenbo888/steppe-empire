# Steppe Empire · 草原帝国

A bilingual (EN / 中文) systems-level platform on nomadic civilizations and steppe empires of Eurasia — from the Scythians to the Qing. Built around mobility, ecology, frontier dynamics, religion, and trade as the recurring drivers of mobile empire formation.

The site is single-page, includes an interactive Eurasian steppe map with toggleable layers (steppe band, climate zones, horse heart, migration corridors, Silk Road), a clickable empires timeline (12 polities), a frontier-pressure matrix, religion-transmission flow cards, a military-protocols section, a climate-correlation panel, and a six-dial empire simulator that maps any configuration onto a known historical archetype.

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, vanilla JS. Inline SVG for the steppe map and the empires timeline. Cormorant Garamond + Source Serif 4 + Noto Serif SC + JetBrains Mono.

## Modules

1. Eurasian Steppe Map Engine — layered, interactive
2. Nomadic Civilization System — pastoralism, kinship, mobile court, oral law
3. Horse & Mobility Revolution — five technologies that built every steppe empire
4. Major Steppe Empires — Scythian, Xiongnu, Hun, Göktürk, Uyghur, Khitan/Liao, Jurchen/Jin, Mongol, Timurid, Crimean, Zunghar, Manchu/Qing
5. Frontier Dynamics — sedentary anchors and the move-set against them
6. Silk Road & Trade Layer — what flowed which way, who ran the routers
7. Religion & Cultural Transmission — Buddhism, Manichaeism, Nestorianism, Islam, Tibetan Buddhism
8. Military Systems Laboratory — feigned retreat, Parthian shot, decimal command, strategic terror
9. Environmental & Ecological Engine — climate–empire correlations
10. Simulator — six dials → four outputs → historical archetype
11. Legacy & Modern Influence — Russia, China's three frontiers, Central Asian identity, Eurasianism
